## Doom's voice

- doom_forfeit, doom_mohith, doom_iam, doom_collect: cut from one take the site owner generated on his own ElevenLabs
  account (ElevenLabs_2026-10-19T02_34_05, long_ivc v3), then lightly shaped with ffmpeg: a short comb resonance (the
  mask), a cold reverb tail, gentle compression and loudness normalisation.

## Score

- doom_score: an original cue for the reveal, synthesised in code for this film (scripts/doom_score.py): sub drone,
  a detuned low string cluster, a heartbeat drum, a formant "choir" pad, and a low hit with a brass-like chord on
  "I am Doom". No samples.
